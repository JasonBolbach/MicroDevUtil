Events and workflow

From finger or knob to your callback

Encoder ISR / button debounce          touch panel (lcd.getTouch)
        \                                   |
         SuperEditIO  <--- Inject keys / steps --- SuperForm::PumpTouch -> OnPointer()
              |
      control.Service()  -> changes its working copy -> IO.Changed()  => OnChange
              |
      A long / lift      -> commits the copy          -> IO.Accepted() => OnAccept
              |
      SuperForm::SetOnEdit(fn)  fn(control, EDIT_ACCEPTED | EDIT_CANCELLED, user)

Three places to listen

WhereGranularityBest for
Control SetOnChangeEvery live change of one controlPreviews, live read-outs
Control SetOnAcceptOne control, once per accepted editSaving a setting
Form SetOnEditAny control, accepted or cancelledOne central handler, page switching, logging

Instant controls

Check box, radio button and push button act in StartEdit() and report EDIT_ACCEPTED on the next Service(); their OnChange and OnAccept fire together.

Animation clocks

SuperControl::Tick() is driven by SuperForm::Service(). The gauge glide and MinMax expiry and the user-draw auto-redraw use it; without a form call the control's Update() from loop().

Keep callbacks short

Callbacks run inside Service(), i.e. inside the UI loop. Set a flag or store a value; do not block, and do not open a modal control from inside another control's OnChange.
